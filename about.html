About BMI Health Tracker

BMI Health Tracker is a simple web application that calculates Body Mass Index (BMI) based on height and weight.

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index is a commonly used screening measurement based on a person's weight and height.

BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 - Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 - Normal weight
  • 25 to 29.9 - Overweight
  • 30 and above - Obesity

BMI is a general screening measure and is not a diagnosis of individual health.
